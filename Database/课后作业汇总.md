### 7. 设有下列关系：

$R(A,\ B,\ C,\ D)$

| A  | B  | C  | D  |
|----|----|----|-----|
| a1 | b1 | c1 | d1 |
| a1 | b1 | c1 | d2 |
| a2 | b2 | c2 | d1 |
| a2 | b3 | c2 | d2 |
| a2 | b1 | c2 | d3 |
| a3 | b2 | c2 | d1 |
| a3 | b2 | c3 | d2 |
| a4 | b3 | c2 | d1 |
| a4 | b3 | c2 | d3 |
| a4 | b1 | c2 | d4 |
| a4 | b4 | c2 | d2 |

$S(D,\ E,\ F)$

| D  | E  | F  |
|----|----|----|
| d1 | e2 | f1 |
| d2 | e1 | f2 |
| d2 | e2 | f3 |
| d3 | e3 | f1 |

$T(D,\ F,\ G)$

| D  | F  | G  |
|----|----|----|
| d1 | f1 | g1 |
| d2 | f2 | g2 |
| d3 | f1 | g3 |

**(1) 求下列关系表达式的值：**

$$E_1 = \Pi_{C,D}(\sigma_{A > \text{'a1'} \wedge B < \text{'b4'}}(R))$$

$$E_2 = \Pi_{A,B,E,G}(\sigma_{A < \text{'a3'} \wedge E < \text{'e3'} \wedge G \neq \text{'g3'}}(R \bowtie S \bowtie T))$$

$$E_3 = R \div \Pi_D(\sigma_{F=\text{'f1'}}(T))$$

$$E_4 = \{t \mid (\exists u)(\exists v)(\exists w)(R(u) \wedge S(v) \wedge T(w) \wedge u[3]>\text{'c1'} \wedge v[2] \neq \text{'e2'} \wedge w[3] \neq \text{'g2'}$$
$$\wedge\ u[4]=v[1] \wedge v[3]>w[2] \wedge t[1]=u[2] \wedge t[2]=u[3] \wedge t[3]=v[1] \wedge t[4]=w[3] \wedge t[5]=w[2])\}$$

**(2) 试将 $E_4$ 转化为等价的关系代数表达式。**
